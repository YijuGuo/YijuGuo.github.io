LENS: Less Noise, More Voice — Our ACL 2026 Paper

2026-04RLVRLLM ReasoningACL 2026

Motivation

Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) has advanced LLM reasoning, but remains constrained by inefficient exploration under limited rollout budgets. We discovered that many exploration failures arise not from problem difficulty, but from a small number of prompt tokens that introduce interference.

Method

We propose the Less Noise Sampling Framework (LENS), a two-stage approach:

Stage 1: Interference Token Identification & Purification

By quantifying the token-level deviation between the policy model and reference model via Interference Score, we precisely identify and remove tokens that mislead reasoning, boosting single-batch sampling success rate by approximately 20%.

Stage 2: Calibrated Sampling Strategy Optimization

Successful reasoning chains from purified prompts are used as supervision signals to calibrate policy updates on the original noisy prompts, improving reasoning robustness under real-world interference.

Results

  • Average +3.88% performance gain across 7 math reasoning benchmarks
  • 1.6x faster convergence compared to GRPO
  • Outperforms both scaling exploration (GRPO with doubled rollouts) and prompt filtering baselines (DAPO, GRESO)
  • Evaluated on Llama-3.2, Qwen-2.5/3, and Qwen-3 model families
